FAQ

What is MINAMI AI Studio?
MINAMI AI Studio is a collection of AI-powered editing tools that you can use before licensing and downloading content.
Do I have to use MINAMI AI Studio?
No, it's completely optional. You can browse, license, and download content without ever using the AI Studio tools.
Do I need a MINAMI subscription?
A free account lets you browse and preview. A subscription unlocks unlimited downloads, AI Studio features, and commercial licensing.
How do I access MINAMI AI Studio?
Sign in to your MINAMI account and click the "AI Studio" button on any image or video detail page.
