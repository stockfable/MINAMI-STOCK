Upload Conditions

Please read these conditions before you upload images or videos to MINAMI STOCK. Every file you submit must follow them.

1. File requirements

  • Images: JPG, PNG or WEBP.
  • Videos: MP4, MOV or WEBM, up to 300 MB each.
  • Minimum file size: every file must be at least 2 MB. Files smaller than 2 MB cannot be uploaded.
  • Minimum resolution: an image must be at least 2000 pixels on its longer side (width or height). Smaller images are rejected.
  • Files must open correctly and must not be corrupted.

2. Ownership and rights

  • Upload only content that you created and that you fully own.
  • Do not upload files copied, downloaded or taken from other websites, photographers or artists.
  • Do not include logos, brand names, trademarks, copyrighted characters, artworks or watermarks that belong to someone else.
  • You confirm that you own all rights to each file when you submit it.

3. People and property

  • If a file shows a recognizable person, you must have a signed model release.
  • If a file shows private property that needs permission, you must have a property release.
  • Tick the correct release options on the upload page.

4. AI-generated content

  • If a file was made with generative AI tools, you must tick the AI option.
  • Do not present AI-generated files as real photographs or real events.

5. Title, keywords and category

  • Title: required, clear and accurate, up to 200 characters.
  • Keywords: at least 5 and at most 50, separated by commas, and relevant to the file.
  • Category: choose the category that fits the file best.
  • Do not use misleading, repeated or spam keywords, and do not use other people's names or brands.

6. Quality

  • Images must be sharp, well exposed and free of heavy noise.
  • No borders, frames, text overlays or watermarks.
  • Videos must be steady, clear and properly lit.

7. Not allowed

  • Illegal content or content that supports illegal activity.
  • Hateful, violent, sexual or abusive content.
  • Misleading or false content.
  • Content that harms, exploits or invades the privacy of others.
If a file contains mature content, you must tick the mature content option when you upload it.

8. Review and removal

  • Every file is reviewed before it is shown on the site. Its status stays pending until it is approved.
  • Files that break these conditions can be rejected or removed.
  • Repeated or serious violations can lead to the loss of upload access.
